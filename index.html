Inside the watch

Four stages, one watch

  1. Stage I

    Crown

    Where your hand meets the movement.

    Unscrew the crown and the stem draws out with it. It is the one part of the watch you touch every day, and the only way in from outside.

    • Crown
    • Stem

  2. Stage II

    Movement

    Built to be looked at.

    With the caseback off, the movement lifts out in one piece and comes apart: screws first, then the bridges, then the wheels beneath them. Each watch is finished by hand, and the sapphire caseback keeps that work in view.

    • Bridges
    • Balance wheel
    • Pallet fork
    • Escape wheel
    • Train wheels
    • Barrel
    • Jewels

  3. Stage III

    Dial

    The face, set apart.

    Lift the hands and the dial falls away: a thin plate carrying the markers, the minute track and the small seconds. It is the part of the watch everyone else sees.

    • Dial
    • Hands
    • Applied markers
    • Small seconds

  4. Stage IV

    Strap

    Back together, back on the wrist.

    Every part returns to where it belongs and the strap closes the circle. It is the last thing fitted and the first thing you feel.

    • Strap
    • Lugs

The workshop

A bench in Bandra

Orrin Watch Co. was founded in Bandra, Mumbai, in 2019 by two independent watchmakers.

They make five mechanical models and finish each one by hand. The caseback is sapphire so the work inside stays visible after the watch is closed.

If you would like to see one in person, write to the workshop.
