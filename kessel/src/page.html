Scroll to open the iris

01 · Iris

Light waits for the blades

The iris sits in the middle of the lens. Scroll and it opens one blade at a time until light floods the glass.

02 · Glass

Through the glass

Each element sits at its own distance from the next. Here they slide apart so the camera can pass between them, through the iris and out at the back.

03 · Back together

Out the back, closed up

Past the mount the camera turns round and the lens closes up behind it, element by element, until it is whole again.

04 · Focus

Focus by hand

The knurled ring turns and the engraved distance scale rolls past the index mark, from infinity down to the closest mark.

05 · Lenses

Three lenses

The names and roles below are proposals for this concept.

  1. Shaniwar proposal

    The wide one. For rooms, streets and landscapes, where everything in front of you belongs in the frame.

  2. Parvati proposal

    The standard one. Frames a scene close to the way your eye does.

  3. Sinhagad proposal

    The long one. For faces and details at a distance, with the background let go.

06 · The workshop

One optician, one lens

Kessel Optics was founded in Pune in 2017 as an independent lens maker.

Every lens is assembled by hand by one optician from start to finish, and that optician signs the barrel.
